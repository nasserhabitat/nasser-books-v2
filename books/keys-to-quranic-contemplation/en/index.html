Keys to Quranic Contemplation: Al-Mathani and the Engineering of Meaning

A revised and expanded edition of “Towards Conscious Contemplation.” A practical guide connecting Qur’anic reading with action, distinguishing al-Mathānī, the Seven Mathānī, and the Mathānī system, and providing examples and exercises to test conclusions using the QCROS methodology, with a dedicated ninth chapter on Ulū al-Albāb, conscious listening, and following the best of speech.
