Opening of the book

Edition note. Adapted from the author's Arabic manuscript, al-Shirk: Handasat al-Wahm wa-Tahrir al-Wa'y al-Insani, editorial version v2.6 (2026). This is a condensed English edition, not a verse-by-verse translation of the complete Arabic work. The Arabic manuscript remains the authoritative record of the textual corpus, tests, qualifications, figures and appendices. Qur'anic references are supplied for orientation; where the sense of a verse is described in English, this is explanatory prose, not a certified rendering of its Arabic wording. The author's specified primary Arabic recitation for eventual quotation verification is Warsh from Nafi'. Comprehensive literal verification of the Arabic citations remains open.

The collection is offered without charge and, where available, in multiple digital formats including PDF, HTML, DOCX and TXT. As of early 2026, the author's library comprised 68 volumes—34 in Arabic and 34 in English. That figure is a historical snapshot provided for the manifesto, not a current live inventory. The intention is to support open access, digital preservation and responsible AI-assisted research.

Three boundaries govern the adaptation. First, an Arabic Qur'anic expression must not be confused with the author's modern analytical label. Second, a useful analogy must not be elevated into a claim about the physical or unseen world. Third, a research question that remained OPEN in the Arabic manuscript remains OPEN here. English terms such as operating system, center of gravity, entropy and network are heuristic images, not words that the Qur'an itself uses for these mechanisms.
