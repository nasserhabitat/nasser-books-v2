The Architecture of Revelation and the Formation of Consciousness: Functional Distinctions among Kitāb, Qurʾān, Dhikr, and Furqān

A comprehensive study in Qur’anic linguistic jurisprudence, MATHANI-NET, and QCROS examining the compositional architecture of the Muṣḥaf, critiquing the ten-sūrah restriction while integrating the circular structure of Sūrat al-Shūrā, and analyzing the operational cycle of the four principal terms of revelation (Kitāb, Qurʾān, Dhikr, and Furqān) and the Seven Mathānī.
