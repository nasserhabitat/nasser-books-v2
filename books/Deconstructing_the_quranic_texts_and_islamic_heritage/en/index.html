Deconstructing the Qur’anic Texts and Islamic Heritage: From the Chaos of Literal Fragmentation and the Custody of Tradition to the Architecture of Consciousness and the Investigation Protocol (QCROS)

A foundational study in Qur’anic linguistic jurisprudence addressing both traditional tutelage and context-isolated fragmentation. It establishes an alternative structural framework governed by the QCROS epistemological protocol, original ʿUthmānic manuscript orthography (Rasm), and the Mathānī network (MATHANI-NET), with an applied study of Sūrat al-Ṭūr and the Schools of Knowledge model.
