Executive Summary & Core Framework
1. The Great Epistemological Shift
The central crisis in traditional and modern thought regarding the Quran lies in reducing its profound terms to mere cultural or temporal labels. Conventional readings flatten "Dunya" (often translated as "this world") and "Akhirah" ("the hereafter") into a simple timeline: life now → death → life later.
This research argues that the Quran is not written in a human language (Lughah) that evolves with culture, but in a divine linguistic code (Lisan) —a closed, mathematically precise system where every letter and root carries precise existential geometry. The Uthmanic script itself functions as a visual code describing the fundamental laws of reality.

8. The Unified Model: Existence as a Phase Transition
The existential journey can be summarized in a single formula:
Guidance → Choice → Work → Earning → Soul Recall → Retention/Release → Death → Interval → Resurrection → Emergence → Gathering → Reckoning → Recompense → Return
But this is not a chronological narrative extracted from a single verse; it is a synthetic framework drawn from the entire network of Quranic usage.
9. The Call to Ascent
This book is not merely a theoretical study. It is a practical invitation:
"You were brought into existence to ascend, not to be confined; to build, not to corrupt; to witness the truth, not to drown in illusions. Choose your path, build your existence, and know that you are the engineer of your destiny between the path of guidance and the path of deviation."
The four tasks of ascent:
Open the Spirit (Ruh)—Reconnect to the Source.
Purify the Soul (Nafs)—Clear accumulated obscurities.
Activate the Intellect ('Aql)—Bind causes to higher norms.
Discipline the Body (Badan)—Make it an obedient instrument of transcendent consciousness.
10. What This Book Does Not Claim
The research acknowledges its limits:
The mechanism of identity continuity between phases is not detailed in the text.
The nature of the body in the Next Genesis remains in the domain of model, not text.
The precise relationship between soul and body in resurrection is not specified.
The detailed nature of Barzakh remains open.
Unresolved mechanisms of recompense remain subject to further induction.
