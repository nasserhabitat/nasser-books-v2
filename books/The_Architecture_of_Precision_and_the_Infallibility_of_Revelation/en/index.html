The Architecture of Precision and the Infallibility of Revelation: From the Illusion of Abrogation (Naskh) to the Journey of Elucidation and Detail in Qur’anic Linguistic Jurisprudence

A foundational and applied study deconstructing the traditional doctrine of nullifying abrogation (Naskh) and consensus claims through contextual analysis and Reverse Stress Testing (QCROS). It establishes the intra-Qur’anic meaning of N-S-Kh as transcription and elucidation, refutes abrogation by solitary reports, and demonstrates the permanent validity (Iḥkām) of the five disputed verses and al-Suyūṭī’s twenty-two verses.
