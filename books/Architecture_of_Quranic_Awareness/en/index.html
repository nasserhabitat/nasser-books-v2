The Architecture of Qur’anic Awareness: Functional and Operational Meanings in the Language of Revelation

A structural and operational reading that moves from static exegesis to systems engineering governed by the Core-and-Functional-Extension principle and QCROS. It explores the inner cognitive landscape, intellectual nourishment, family and kinship terms, generative continuity (walad, banūn, abnāʾ), and the ethics of privacy, awareness, and shared resources in Sūrat al-Nūr.
