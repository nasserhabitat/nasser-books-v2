EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Central Question

"Does strategic deception possess the power of lasting domination? Or is it a closed system that carries within its very design the seeds of its own self-destruction?"

This work investigates deception (al-makr) not as a mere moral failing or tactical trick, but as a structural and linguistic phenomenon governed by immutable cosmic laws (sunan) embedded in the Quranic system. Through a rigorous deconstruction of the Arabic root (م-ك-ر / m-k-r), the book reveals why every deception scheme—from ancient tribal conspiracies to modern cyber warfare—inevitably collapses upon its architects.

CHAPTER ONE: THE LINGUISTIC-STRUCTURAL FOUNDATION

The Integrated Mathani Methodology

The foundational premise of this study is that the triliteral root in Arabic is not an arbitrary phonetic unit but a kinetic-energetic system formed by the fusion of two binary morphemes. When the root (مَكَرَ / makara) is deconstructed, we uncover a two-part movement:

مَكَرَ (makara)=مَكْ (mak)_(⏟)_(Hidden Empowerment)+كَرَّ (karra)_(⏟)_(Circular Rebound)

The First Morpheme: (مَكْ / mak) – Hidden Empowerment

Phono-Kinetic Origin: The combination of the consonant mīm (denoting gathering, containment, and comprehensive embrace) with the consonant kāf (denoting intensity, bending, pressure, and reversion) generates a constructive movement that can be described as: "intensive pressure on a specific point, extracting its essence, and establishing its center in depth, away from the surface of perception."

Linguistic Evidence:

makka al-‘azma: "he extracted the bone marrow completely"—an act of thorough extraction and deep empowerment.

tamkīn: "firm establishment"—rooting foundations and solidifying positions.

Makkah: the sacred city—a name evoking containment, spatial rootedness, and the concentration of a central axis.

The Second Morpheme: (كَرَّ / karra) – Circular Rebound

Phono-Kinetic Origin: The combination of the consonant kāf (intensity and bending) with the consonant rā' (repetition, vibration, and flow) produces a movement of "bending back upon itself, returning repeatedly to the point of origin, and encircling the axis."

Linguistic Evidence:

al-karr (as opposed to al-farr): the return to attack after retreat—the circular repetition of combat movement.

al-kurrah: the complete cycle that begins at a point and returns to it (like a ball of thread, a game ball, or a cycle of time).

karra al-sharīta: "he folded the tape back upon its axis"—a gesture of rolling and reversion.

The Unified Structural Equation

When the two morphemes unite, the complete kinetic equation of the root emerges as a three-phase sunnanic sequence:

The Hidden Building Phase (مَكْ / mak): The deceiver weaves threads in secrecy, establishing the foundations of his plot in depth, believing that he has cloaked his work in absolute secrecy and precision.

The Apparent Launch Phase: The deceiver believes his scheme proceeds in a straight line toward harming the target, oblivious to the circular dimension of movement.

The Rebound and Encirclement Phase (كَرَّ / karra): The suppressed energy of deception begins to return and bend upon itself by virtue of its structural properties; the foundations laid in secret transform into a trap that closes upon their maker, and the threads of conspiracy curl back to encircle the deceiver's own neck.
